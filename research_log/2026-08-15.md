## EXP14 task-space candidates and benchmark correction

EXP14 wave began with 1,183.44 GB free. Eight object/task-space routes produced 632 calibration candidates with no clipping, but Drawer/Bowl still had 0/8 expert-improvement groups and calibration terminal failure was 46.36%. Formal run `exp14_s4_formal_candidates_20260815` confirmed 14/84 opportunities, all Stove (14/28), with 100% candidate validity.

The failure exposed a benchmark ceiling: Drawer expert nominal quality was 6.0 and the best successful candidate gap was 0.00188; Bowl's best successful gap was 0.03888, below the frozen 0.05 gate. EXP15 therefore hides target-demo future actions from candidates, keeps them only as an evaluation upper bound, and evaluates reference-free feedback/recovery candidates relative to a frozen non-oracle default. This preserves action-consequence coordination while creating deployment-realistic decision demand.
