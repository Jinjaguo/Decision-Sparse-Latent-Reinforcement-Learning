# Next experiment from EXP15

EXP15 created the first target-future-free feedback candidate sets. Calibration achieved 62.5% safe-candidate availability and 58.3% decision demand, proving that the revised benchmark creates a real selection problem. Bowl was solved by every route, while Drawer reached 4/8 and Stove 3/8.

The frozen 200 N safety stop blocked 20–42% of several routes and almost every Stove rollout, but it was never calibrated against successful Stove expert contact. EXP16 first measures successful expert force envelopes, then combines task-specific progress features, monotone/persistent retrieval, conservative ensembles, phase-guarded retargeting, action smoothing and recovery specialists. The goal is not merely to raise a threshold; it is to make safety comparable to valid contact-rich behavior and improve the underlying policies.

The same reference-free recovery gates remain. Passing EXP16 authorizes consequence-coordinator training; failure triggers a stronger policy/planner source rather than stopping.
