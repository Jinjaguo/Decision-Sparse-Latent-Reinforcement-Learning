# Next experiment from EXP19

Static formal cross-fitting cannot reliably identify complementary rescue routes: the best EXP19 variant recovered 45.71% of demand groups. EXP20 therefore creates independent outcome supervision by running the same candidates on EXP8 training demos under strict leave-one-demo-out retrieval. It adds full initial-chunk and retrieval descriptors, trains without formal outcomes, and evaluates once on EXP17 formal candidates. Utility gates remain unchanged.
