## Held-out selection audit

Deterministic top-1 selection from the full factorized score achieved utility
0.950 across 60 held-out branches and selected no unsafe candidate. The
route-blind factorized score achieved utility 0.933. This supports a modest
state-dependent route-selection contribution, but the difference uses a
small, highly favorable development matrix and deterministic tie breaking;
the tie-aware R6 metrics remain the primary comparison.

Route identity was not fitted as an outcome-derived prior. It was an explicit
candidate identity available before action, and all routewise outcome profiles
are reported as audit labels only.
