# Next experiment from EXP16

EXP16 corrected the force-stop confound and passed calibration. Formal evaluation reached 78.57% safe availability, 47.62% decision demand and 100% candidate validity. Bowl was 100% available and Stove 89.29%; demand recovery was 55%, narrowly below the 60% gate because Drawer recovered only 3 of 18 demand groups.

The Drawer error is strongly horizon-structured. Early branches have 89–125 expert steps remaining, but all candidates stop at 80. Most recovered branches are later states with 40–74 remaining steps. EXP17 prospectively sets a target-independent 140-step recovery horizon and compares multiple weighted, median, medoid, smoothed, monotone and persistent retrieval policies. Gates remain unchanged.
