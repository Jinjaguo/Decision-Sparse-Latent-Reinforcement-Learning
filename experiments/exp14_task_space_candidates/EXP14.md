# EXP14 — Object-Centric Task-Space Candidate Planning

EXP14 replaces local action-space editing with object-centric proposal intent.
It compares temporal lookahead, direct EEF waypoints, object-frame directions,
cross-demo geometric retargeting, local inverse response, semantic skills,
guided selection, and restricted compositions. Candidate plans are frozen before
simulator outcomes and executed through the corrected-D EXP11 engine.
